Phantom Phase seccomp policy
============================

The binary installs a 19-instruction classic BPF filter with PR_SET_SECCOMP.
After verifying AUDIT_ARCH_X86_64, it permits only these syscall numbers:

0    read
1    write
3    close
15   rt_sigreturn
60   exit
231  exit_group
257  openat

Every other syscall returns SECCOMP_RET_KILL_PROCESS. The final exploit uses
only openat, read, write, and exit.
